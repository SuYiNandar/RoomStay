How is the database designed?

People and rooms

  • User — renter, partner, or admin
  • PartnerApplication — who may list rooms
  • Property — the room, including facilities
  • PropertyPhoto, Favorite, Viewing

Booking records

  • Booking — the request and its status
  • ReservationDeposit — amount, deadline, and the slip
  • Conversation and Message
  • Review, Report, Notification, AuditLog

ROOMSTAY

Facilities stay on Property

facilities: string[]

Wi-Fi, Air conditioner, Furnished, Parking, and the rest are one list on the property row.

One table

The ERD and the database use the same Property table.

Shown in the GUI

Room page chips and the partner listing form read that list.

No extra tables

Facility and PropertyFacility are not in this database.

How the system flows

6

Move in

The room is rented, then available again when the rental ends.
